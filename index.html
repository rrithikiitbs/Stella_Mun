About the Conference

A stage for those who speak first and listen better.

Stella MUN is a student-run Model United Nations conference bringing together delegates from schools and colleges for two days of formal debate, crisis diplomacy, and resolution-writing. Stella MUN 2027 continues that tradition with six committees spanning historical crisis, contemporary politics, economics, and international press.
